# Roadmap

kiln powers [hakula.xyz](https://hakula.xyz). Its scope follows the site's publishing needs, with CJK technical writing and understandable engine design as priorities.

## Current Status

Supported capabilities and their usage guides are listed in the [README](../README.md#highlights). [IgnIt](https://github.com/hakula139/IgnIt) owns the reader interface and its presentation features.

## Current Focus

Review and simplify the existing engine before extending it. Priorities are coherent ownership of routes and assets, reliable publication, shared rendering contracts, and behavioral tests that catch failures at those boundaries.

## Later

A demo site showing the supported authoring and publishing workflow. Further engine work should follow concrete publishing needs.

## Scope

Full Hugo compatibility and separate per-language site trees remain outside the current plan.
